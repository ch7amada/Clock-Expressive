Clock Expressive is an unofficial fork of Fossify Clock redesigned with Material 3 Expressive. It works offline and is ad-free.

• Clock: See local time, your next alarm, and saved world clocks.
• Alarms: Set recurring days, custom sounds, vibration, and snooze.
• Stopwatch: Track elapsed time and record laps.
• Timers: Save reusable presets and choose custom sounds and vibration.
• Appearance: Choose light or dark mode, system colors, or a curated palette. Add digital or analog home-screen widgets that follow Android’s appearance.
